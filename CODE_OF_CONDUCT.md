# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being kind and patient, particularly with people who are new to this
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Things that do not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. They may
remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not
align with this code of conduct.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private report through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://github.com/dj-skn/notion-bg-gen/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
